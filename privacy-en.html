Privacy Policy

How SecreAI & Tools (https://amachi-tools.win/) handles inquiry data, advertising cookies, analytics, and local data in our software and browser extensions.

Effective Date: November 9, 2026 Last Updated: November 20, 2026

SecreAI & Tools (hereinafter referred to as "this website") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you visit this website or use the software and extensions introduced here.

1. Purpose of Collecting and Using Personal Information

When you contact us through our Contact Form, you may be asked to provide personal information such as your name (or handle) and email address.

Any personal information provided will be used strictly for responding to your inquiries, following up on bug reports, or providing necessary information. We will never use your personal information for any other purpose or disclose it to third parties without your consent, except where required by applicable law.

2. Advertising (Google AdSense)

This website uses "Google AdSense," a third-party advertising service. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve personalized ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites across the web.

  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.
  • For more information on how Google uses data and cookies in advertising, please review Google's Advertising – Privacy & Terms.

3. Analytics Tools

This website may use traffic analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) and standard hosting access metrics (Cloudflare Pages) to understand site usage and improve user experience and content quality.

These services may use cookies or similar technologies to collect traffic data anonymously. No personally identifiable information is collected through analytics. You can disable cookies in your browser settings to refuse data collection.

4. Data Handling in Distributed Software & Chrome Extensions

All desktop tools and Google Chrome extensions developed and distributed via this website (SecreAI, SubtitLocar, TruckerWalkAround, MyNewsReader, YouTube Multi Auto Playlist, and 4 Screen Viewer) are designed with a local-first and privacy-focused architecture.

  • No Collection by the Site Operator: Screen captures, microphone or system audio, AI conversation logs, ChromaDB vector databases, RSS subscriptions, video URLs, and playlist states are stored and processed locally on your device or browser storage. The operator of this website does not collect, inspect, or store any of this data on external servers.
  • Third-Party AI APIs: When you configure your own external API key (such as Gemini API or OpenAI API) in tools like SecreAI, your prompts and captured images are transmitted directly to the respective API provider. Please refer to each provider's privacy policy and terms of service for their data practices. When using local LLMs (e.g., Ollama or LM Studio), all inference remains strictly on your local machine.
  • Chrome Extension Permissions: YouTube Multi Auto Playlist and 4 Screen Viewer request only the minimum browser permissions required for multi-view layout and playlist automation, and do not transmit browsing history or personal data to external servers.

5. External Links and Embedded Content

This website contains links to external platforms and embedded widgets, including GitHub, Google Forms, the Chrome Web Store, VirusTotal, and YouTube. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites. Please review the privacy policy of each external service you visit.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect legal requirements, changes in our tools, or updates to advertising and analytics configurations. Any changes take effect immediately upon publication on this page.
