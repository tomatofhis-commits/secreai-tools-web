Developer's Note

Design Philosophy — Lightweight Multi-POV Streaming Without Playback Barriers

Following collaborative streams, esports tournaments, or breaking news from multiple sources usually forces you to resize separate browser windows or juggle tabs constantly. 4 Screen Viewer was built to eliminate that clutter—launching a dedicated multi-view workspace inside a single tab with just one click on the extension icon.

Running four full video pages concurrently in a browser introduces two real-world challenges: heavy memory consumption from background scripts like live chat and comments, and embedding restrictions (Error 152-4) when using standard embedded players.

In v1.4.0, the extension introduces a hybrid playback engine. By default, Lite (Embed) mode pairs lightweight /embed/ players with declarativeNetRequest Referer optimization to minimize Error 152-4 while cutting memory usage dramatically. Whenever a creator explicitly blocks embedding, a single toggle switches seamlessly to Standard (Watch) mode—backed by full frame destruction and heavy DOM removal to keep your browser responsive.

Key Features

Key Features & Technical Advantages

Engineered for multi-stream convenience, reduced memory footprint during long viewing sessions, and zero interference with normal browsing.

4 Flexible Layouts with Auto-Save

Switch seamlessly between 4 Grid (2x2), 2 Side (1x2), 1 Main + 2 Sub, and 1 Main + 3 Sub. Your layout preference is saved automatically in localStorage so your workspace opens just the way you left it.

Lite (Embed) Memory-Saving Mode

Enabled by default, Lite (Embed) uses lightweight YouTube embed players paired with Referer header optimization to prevent Error 152-4 and cut memory usage to roughly 30–40% of full-page loading. If a video owner disables embedding, simply uncheck Lite (Embed) to reload instantly in full Watch mode.

Deep DOM Cleanup & Frame Disposal

Parent tab initialization calls window.stop() immediately to block unnecessary YouTube homepage scripts, and iframes are created only when loaded. Clearing a slot, toggling modes, or shrinking layouts sets about:blank and calls .remove() to reclaim memory. Even in Watch mode, live chat, comments, and related panels are physically removed from the DOM and autoplay timers stop once active.

Multi-Platform Auto URL Conversion

Paste standard share URLs directly without manual editing. Built-in regex converters recognize YouTube (standard, youtu.be, /live/), Nico Nico Douga, Twitch (channels, VODs, clips), Vimeo, and raw <iframe src="..."> embed snippets.

1-Click Launch, Enter Key & Dark Theater UI

Click the toolbar icon to open the viewer tab immediately. Press Enter right after pasting a URL to load instantly, and enjoy distraction-free playback against a clean dark theme (#0f0f0f) with zero wasted screen space.

Privacy-First & Isolated Sub-Frame Rules

Built on Manifest V3 using scoped declarativeNetRequest rules restricted to sub_frame contexts so regular browser tabs and YouTube live chats remain completely untouched. All URLs and preferences stay local in your browser with zero external tracking.

FAQ & Troubleshooting

Frequently Asked Questions

Solutions for common playback restrictions, memory management tips, and privacy details.

Q1. What should I do if YouTube shows "Error 152-4" or "Video unavailable"?

Some creators disable external embedding on their videos, which blocks playback inside standard /embed/ frames. Simply uncheck Lite (Embed) in the top control bar to switch to Watch theater mode—the extension will reload active slots automatically and bypass the embed restriction.

Q2. Why does a playing video occasionally pause when I load another slot?

Chrome's media focus handling can sometimes pause an existing stream when a new iframe starts autoplaying. You can simply click play to resume, or pause your current stream right before loading a new URL into another slot.

Q3. How can I keep memory usage as low as possible during long streams?

Keep Lite (Embed) checked ON whenever possible, as it avoids loading the heavy YouTube SPA. Also select 2 Side (1x2) or 1 Main + 2 Sub when you do not need all four slots, since hidden slots destroy their iframes automatically. After hours of continuous playback, clicking Clear and Load on a slot resets its buffers cleanly.

Q4. Does this affect my regular YouTube tabs or live chat?

No. Header rules in background.js are scoped strictly to sub_frame contexts, and the UI cleanup script only activates when ?4screen=1 or &subscreen=1 is present in the URL. Your normal YouTube tabs and live chats continue working normally.

Q5. Are my video URLs or viewing history sent to any external server?

Never. Layout and Lite mode preferences are stored strictly inside your browser's localStorage, and all URL parsing runs locally on your machine. No browsing history or personal data is ever transmitted to our servers (see our Privacy Policy).

Q6. How do I manage audio when multiple streams are playing at once?

Each slot hosts an independent player with its own volume and mute controls. When using 1 Main + 3 Sub or 4 Grid (2x2), a practical setup is keeping audio enabled only on your primary slot while muting the sub-screens to monitor visual action.
