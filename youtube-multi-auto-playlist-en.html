Key Features & Benefits

Engineered to give you flexible multitasking, stream monitoring, and hassle-free background watching.

Stream 1 to 4 Videos in Side Window

Display between 1 and 4 video frames side-by-side right inside your browser's side window. Perfect for following multiple live stream perspectives, gaming tournaments, news coverage, and tutorials without overcrowding your main tabs.

Auto-Generate Playlists from Channels

Automatically create ready-to-watch playlists directly from your YouTube subscriptions and channel feeds. Stop wasting time manually finding and queuing each new upload.

Continuous Sequential Auto-Play

Toggle on the "Auto-Play" switch to let the extension seamlessly play through your generated list in order. Great for background music or binge-watching your favorite series uninterrupted.

Important

Recommendations & Usage Notes

Single-Screen Playback (1 Window) is Recommended for Auto-Play

To ensure maximum stability and reliable sequential video transitions, we recommend using 1-screen playback when the Auto-Play feature is turned on.

Stopping Video Frames During Multi-Screen Playback

Stopping or closing other video frames while playing across multiple screens (2 to 4 screens) may negatively affect playback management and cause unexpected behavior.

[Note] Pausing a video directly using the native video player controls will not cause any issues and is completely safe to use.
