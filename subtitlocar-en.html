OBS Studio Live Streaming Setup Guide

Step-by-step instructions to integrate SubtitLocar subtitles as a crisp, transparent overlay onto your OBS Studio stream canvas.

STEP 1

Launch SubtitLocar

Start SubtitLocar, enable your audio capture (microphone or system audio), and connect your local LLM (Ollama or LM Studio). Keep the subtitle display window visible on your screen.

STEP 2

Add Window Capture in OBS

In OBS Studio's "Sources" dock, click the "+" button and select "Window Capture". Select "[SubtitLocar]" from the Window list and set Capture Method to "Windows 10 (1903 and up)".

STEP 3

Apply Color Key Transparency

Right-click the added source and select "Filters". Under Effect Filters, click "+" to add a "Color Key". Choose the matching background color (e.g., green or black) and adjust Similarity to make the background completely transparent.

STEP 4

Position & Start Streaming

Position the subtitle box on your OBS preview canvas (e.g., along the bottom or near your webcam). Hold the Alt key while dragging handles to crop any unnecessary borders. You are ready to stream!

Recommended PC Specs & Model Selection Guide

Practical guidelines for selecting STT (speech-to-text) and local LLM models when streaming games via OBS on the same PC (as of November 2026).

Entry Setup

GPU VRAM 4GB - 6GB / GTX 1660, RTX 3050, 4050, etc.

Recommended STT (Speech-to-Text)
Whisper base - small (faster-whisper)

Minimizes inference latency with the lowest possible resource consumption.

Recommended LLM Model
1.5B - 3B Class (Q4_K_M Quantized)

Examples: Qwen 2.5 1.5B / 3B, Gemma 2 2B

OBS Gaming Guideline:
Suited for lightweight indie titles, 2D platformers, and browser games. When streaming heavier 3D games, offload LLM layers to CPU or stick to a 1.5B model to prevent GPU VRAM exhaustion.
Sweet Spot Recommendation

Mid-Range Setup

GPU VRAM 8GB - 12GB / RTX 3060, 4060, 4060 Ti, etc.

Recommended STT (Speech-to-Text)
Whisper small - medium

Strikes the optimal balance between high transcription accuracy and quick response.

Recommended LLM Model
7B - 9B Class (Q4_K_M Quantized)

Examples: Qwen 2.5 7B, Gemma 2 9B, Llama 3.1 8B

OBS Gaming Guideline:
The standard configuration for streaming popular 3D titles (Apex, VALORANT, Genshin, etc.) on medium-to-high settings. Allocate 6-8GB VRAM to the game and 3-4GB to STT/LLM for smooth, zero-framedrop streaming.

High-End Setup

GPU VRAM 16GB+ / RTX 4070 Ti S, 4080, 4090, Dual-GPU, etc.

Recommended STT (Speech-to-Text)
Whisper medium - large-v3

Industry-leading transcription accuracy, even with fast speech, jargon, and background noise.

Recommended LLM Model
9B - 14B Class (Q4_K_M - Q6_K Quantized)

Examples: Qwen 2.5 14B, Gemma 4 12B, Command R

OBS Gaming Guideline:
Ample headroom for streaming cutting-edge AAA titles at ultra settings. Capable of running SubtitLocar and SecreAI ChromaDB vector searches simultaneously without any latency.
