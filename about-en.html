About SecreAI & Tools

Meet the developer behind SecreAI & Tools, our hands-on hardware testing philosophy, and our commitment to free, local-first software.

1. Site Overview & Mission

SecreAI & Tools (https://amachi-tools.win/) is the official portal for free desktop software, simulator plugins, and Google Chrome extensions independently developed by amachi to make everyday PC workflows, gaming, and live streaming more productive and enjoyable.

Rather than serving merely as a download directory, this website documents practical workflows tested on real hardware—covering local LLM setups (Ollama / LM Studio), Elgato Stream Deck and OBS Studio integrations, hardware spec guidelines, and safe plugin configurations—available in both Japanese and English for users worldwide.

3. Development & Verification Policy

Real-Hardware Verification

All setup guides, recommended GPU/RAM tiers, and integration steps published on this site are grounded in hands-on verification across actual desktop environments, multi-GPU local LLM setups, OBS streaming rigs, and live game sessions.

Local-First Privacy & Zero API Lock-In

Relying solely on cloud APIs brings recurring token costs and privacy concerns. Tools such as SecreAI, SubtitLocar, and MyNewsReader prioritize local LLMs and local vector storage (ChromaDB) so users can run powerful AI workflows privately and free of ongoing fees.

Safety Design & Transparency

Source code is shared openly on GitHub, major releases include VirusTotal scan links, and game tools such as TruckerWalkAround are engineered with automatic launcher cleanup routines to leave game directories clean after exit.

Global Accessibility & Continuous Updates

Because useful utilities know no borders, core documentation is maintained in both Japanese and English, with plans to expand language support and technical guides further based on community feedback.
